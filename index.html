Three things to know before you touch anything

She ages in her sleep. The device deep-sleeps after five minutes, but the clock keeps running. Every duration here is real-world time, not play time.
Her mood reads her worst stat. Not the average. One bottomed-out need makes her critical even if the other four are full.
One hidden number decides everything. The parenting score. It never appears on the device, and it alone picks which branch of the evolution tree she takes.

Mood bands

Parenting score

The hidden number. Born on 75, moves at the rate her current band sets, and is checked once at each evolution fork.

Death clock

Runs only while she is critical, and zeroes the instant she recovers. There is no partial credit.
